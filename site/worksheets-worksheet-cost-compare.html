Part 3 — The break-even reality check

Question Example Yours
Total planned spend? $139
Selling price you set for the primary format? $16.99 pb / $9.99 eb
Your per-unit on that format after platform cut? ~$7.89 (Amazon pb) / ~$6.84 (ebook)
Break-even units = spend ÷ per-unit ~18–21 copies
Is that a realistic first-year number for this title? Yes for a niche guide
Have I promised anyone an exclusive right that outlives break-even? No
Any price conflict? (same ISBN, different list prices on two platforms) No

Part 4 — Five-year intuition (rough compounding)

Channel Year 1 Year 3 if the series parents 2 more books Why
Amazon only steady steady × ~2.5 (new titles) New titles carry the old ones
+ Wide + libraries slow start keeps printing from 300 library systems indefinitely Reference titles never die
+ Direct store list-dependent the only channel where old titles earn at launch margin Your email list compounds
+ Audio zero to negative (upfront) pays after break-even; 40% exclusive vs wide share Narrator relationships outlive books

The whole point of the wide channels is that they cost the least effort after set-up and never have a bad month. Set them up carefully once; check them once a year.
