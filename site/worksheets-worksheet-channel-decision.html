Worksheet: channel decision (six questions, one answer)

For one title, at one moment. Score each of the six questions, then read the verdict line at the bottom. Ten minutes, pen or terminal.

Title: __________________________ Date: ____________

Q1 — Who already buys my stuff? (audience gravity)

Situation Points
An email list or in-person following that answers when I say "buy" +3
Followers who like my posts but rarely move +1
Nobody yet; strangers find me through a search engine or store only 0

Q2 — What does the buyer's format look like? (format gravity)

Situation Points
My readers want ebooks and will pay full price Direct-store leaning (+2)
My readers mostly browse Amazon anyway (they found the last book there) Amazon-only leaning (+1)
My readers are nursing students / professionals who print things Paperback-leaning (+2)
My content is reference, not narrative — people may prefer a library copy Library-leaning (+1)

Q3 — How much cash can I put behind one title, honestly?

Situation Points
$500+ available and this is a flagship title PFH audio / owned ISBNs (+2)
$100–500, cover already paid ISBN 10-pack + IngramSpark (+1)
$0 — this book needs to earn before it costs ACX royalty share + free ISBN (+2)

Q4 — Does a physical place want this book?

(signings, a niche shop, conference, guild event, local library shelf)

Situation Points
Yes — named place with a date IngramSpark (+3)
Plausible but unconfirmed IngramSpark later (+1)
No realistic physical-space reader Skip brick-and-mortar paths (0)

Q5 — Does the rights trade hurt? (audio branch)

Situation Points
I want my audiobook on my own site too Exclusive ACX costs me that (+1 against exclusive)
Seven years locked is fine if Audible is 90% of my audio sales Exclusive is fine (+1 for exclusive)
I might want to re-narrate with AI or a new narrator later Non-exclusive (+2 for non-exclusive)

Q6 — What is the next book? (series gravity)

Situation Points
Sequel or companion planned within 12 months Invest in owned ISBNs + direct store (audience compounds) (+2)
This might be my only book Minimise cash, use free ISBN + royalty share (0)
Known bundle (book + worksheet pack) coming Direct store overlaps almost mandatory (+2)

Read your own verdict

Score pattern Classic verdict
Low audience (Q1=0), no cash (Q3 low) Amazon-first + ACX royalty share (non-exclusive) + free ISBN. Come back after 300 emails.
Some audience, cash available, named physical place Owned ISBNs + IngramSpark at 40% + direct store. Amazon stays open; audio waits for proof.
Big list, flagship title, series plan Everything wide: owned ISBNs, IngramSpark, PFH audio when proven, direct store running from launch.
Reference book, many niches, slow income taste Library doors first: D2D wide + library tick, Indie Author Project, Palace. Amazon stays the gateway.

Tally, then flip to the cost table in the cost-compare worksheet to put dollars on the verdict before spending anything.
