# Start here: publishing beyond KDP, in plain English

by Asdale Dynamics, LLC — companion to **kdp-profit-planner** (the math) and
**kdp-interior-templates** (the book files).

You already know how to publish on Amazon KDP. This guide is about the rest of
the map: what happens when your book sells outside Amazon — and, just as
important, what stays yours when it does.

Everything here is written like a friend would explain it. No jargon without a
translation. No decision without a number to look at. All numbers in this
guide are **round, realistic estimates for planning** — the real fees on any
platform's page always win. Where something changes often (setup fees, promo
codes), this guide tells you that instead of pretending to be current news.

**This is educational information, not legal, tax, or financial advice.**
For anything you would have to sign, owe money on, or swear under oath, run it
past a professional who has seen your actual paperwork.

## The one-paragraph version of the whole guide

- **Copyright is automatic.** The moment you write your book, you own it.
  Registration (about $45–65) is optional but buys real legal muscle in the US.
- **ISBNs are just barcodes with metadata.** Free ones from KDP are fine for
  Kindle-only thinking; owned ISBNs from Bowker (about $125 each, cheaper in
  bulk) keep your imprint name under your control everywhere.
- **KDP's expanded distribution is the slow lane.** IngramSpark is the faster
  lane to bookstores and libraries — at the cost of setup fees and wholesale
  discounts.
- **Audiobooks have four doors**, from "narrator splits royalties with you,
  you pay nothing" to cheap AI narration to recording it yourself.
- **Libraries buy in through separate doors** than bookstores — and they pay
  far more per copy.
- **Direct sales are the profit ceiling.** 85–95% of the cover price against
  Amazon's ~60% — if you can bring your own readers.
- **Taxes are just arithmetic.** Roughly 15% self-employment tax plus your
  income-tax rate on net profit, deductible along the way, payable quarterly.

## How this guide was built

The whole site (this document set) is generated by a small Python script from
plain Markdown files in `docs/` — no network, no accounts, no tracking. To
rebuild or edit it yourself:

```bash
python3 build.py          # renders docs/*.md into site/*.html
python3 -m pytest tests/  # verifies the build actually works
```

Open `site/index.html` in any browser. That is the entire setup.
